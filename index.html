About Me

I’m Ciprian, a digital media graduate with experience in content creation, social media, and e-commerce. I’ve worked on everything from product listings in Magento to web banners, photography, and video editing. I enjoy the mix of creative work and practical tasks that comes with bringing a project together.

I like figuring things out as I go, trying different approaches, and working with what I have. I’m still exploring where I want to take my career, but creating visual content is something I keep coming back to.
